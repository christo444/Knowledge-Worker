# Contract with Blue Harbor Clinics for SignalBoard

## Terms

1. **Parties**: This agreement is between Northstar Works ("Provider") and
   Blue Harbor Clinics ("Client").
2. **Scope**: Provider will configure SignalBoard for approved service metrics,
   create two scheduled reports, and train up to five administrators.
3. **Payment**: Client will pay $900 per month, billed electronically.
4. **Duration**: The initial term is six months from the service start date.

## Data Access

Client administrators may view aggregated service metrics. Access to
customer-level details requires a separate role approval from the Client.

## Renewal

The agreement may be renewed by mutual written confirmation at least fifteen
days before the term ends. Any pricing change must be stated in the renewal
notice.

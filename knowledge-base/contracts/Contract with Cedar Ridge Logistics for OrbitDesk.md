# Contract with Cedar Ridge Logistics for OrbitDesk

## Terms

1. **Parties**: This agreement is between Northstar Works ("Provider") and
   Cedar Ridge Logistics ("Client").
2. **Scope**: Provider will configure OrbitDesk for three operations teams,
   including workflow setup, user onboarding, and standard reporting.
3. **Payment**: Client will pay $650 per month, billed at the beginning of each
   monthly service period.
4. **Duration**: The initial term is twelve months from the service start date.

## Support

Provider supplies business-hours support through the customer portal. Critical
service interruptions are handled under the incident response process.

## Renewal

The agreement renews for another twelve-month term unless either party gives
written notice at least thirty days before the current term ends.

# OrbitDesk: Operations Workspace

## Summary

OrbitDesk is a shared workspace for teams that coordinate recurring operational
tasks. It combines work queues, ownership rules, service-level timers, and
auditable activity history in one interface.

## Features

- **Work Queues**: Route incoming tasks to teams using configurable rules.
- **Ownership Rules**: Assign clear owners and backup contacts for every item.
- **Service Timers**: Track response and completion targets with reminders.
- **Activity History**: Preserve status changes, comments, and handoffs.
- **Reports**: Export weekly workload and response-time summaries.

## Plans

- **Team**: $180 per month for up to 15 users.
- **Business**: $650 per month for up to 60 users, including advanced reports.
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing with dedicated support and data-retention options.

## Roadmap

The next planned release adds calendar-based workload planning and regional
business-hour support.

# Operating Policies

## Support Hours

Customer support operates Monday through Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 Pacific
Time. Critical incidents may be reported at any time through the incident
number listed in the customer portal.

## Data Handling

Customer data must be accessed only for a documented business purpose. Team
members must use the company password manager, enable multi-factor
authentication, and report suspected exposure to the security lead immediately.

## Remote Work

Employees may work from an approved location after confirming that the
workspace has reliable internet access and reasonable privacy. Company
equipment must not be left unattended in public places.

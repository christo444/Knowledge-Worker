# Northstar Works Overview

Northstar Works is a fictional software company that builds workflow tools for
distributed operations teams. The company was founded in 2021 and operates
remotely across North America and Europe.

## Mission

Northstar Works helps teams turn scattered operational information into clear,
repeatable processes. Its products focus on visibility, collaboration, and
measurable service quality.

## Organization

The company has four departments:

- Product and Engineering
- Customer Operations
- Sales and Partnerships
- People and Finance

The main office address is 455 Harbor Street, Portland, Oregon. The company
also maintains small coworking memberships in Toronto and Dublin.

## Working Principles

- Document decisions so work can continue asynchronously.
- Prefer simple workflows over unnecessary process.
- Protect customer information and limit access by role.
- Measure outcomes rather than activity alone.

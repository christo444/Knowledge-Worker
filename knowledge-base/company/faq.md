# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do customers contact support?

Customers can open a ticket from the Northstar Works portal or email
support@northstar.example. The support team acknowledges normal requests
within one business day.

## How often are products updated?

The platform receives routine updates every two weeks. Planned maintenance is
announced at least three business days in advance.

## Where should partnership requests go?

Potential partners should contact partnerships@northstar.example with a short
description of their service, target customers, and proposed integration.
